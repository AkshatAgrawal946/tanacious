WAPE on hidden readings, lower is better · Real campus: 7 meters, Building Data Genome Project 2, CC BY-SA 4.0

This is the synergy proof, measured rather than claimed. Same buildings, same hidden readings, same bill reconciliation; only the shape changes. Human only uses the facilities schedules scaled to each bill. AI only is the same Random Forest given weather, calendar and clock time but none of the human knowledge. Human plus AI is Tanacious. It beats both on all 5 synthetic buildings and all 7 real meters with 30% of days hidden, 12 of 12. With a building fully unmetered it still wins on 6 of 7; the exception is the real library, where the human estimate alone did slightly better, 28.6 versus 32.5 percent. On the 4–8 PM window that matters most, error falls to 2.5 percent, against 6.2 for AI alone and 13.6 for human alone.
