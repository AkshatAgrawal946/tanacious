02 · Proposed solution

Three steps from bill to 15-minute curve

1

Learn the shape

A Random Forest learns from schedule, weekends, breaks and heat. No clock time.

2

Match the bill

Each month adds up to its utility bill, within 0.01 kWh.

3

Keep real readings

Meter data comes back unchanged. Only the gaps are filled.

The model trains on every real 15-minute reading: smart meters plus temporary pilot meters. We left clock time out on purpose, so it follows each building type’s own routine instead of copying the metered buildings’ timing. The bill sets the absolute level. Real readings change by 0 kWh, and our tests check that.
