03 · Proof of synergy · what each side brings

People bring the routine, AI learns the response

Human

Building schedules, weekend and vacation habits, the academic calendar, the bill.

Alone: 24% error. Rough guesses miss the weather and the real timing.

AI

Learns from every 15-minute reading how load bends with heat, weekends and breaks.

Alone: 16% error. It copies the metered buildings’ timing onto every other type.

Human + AI

People set the routine and the total. AI learns how the load responds.

Together: 9.7% error, better than either alone on every building tested.

The split of work. The facilities team knows things no meter records: each building type’s rough daily routine, how busy it stays at weekends and in vacations, the academic calendar, and the monthly bill. The AI, a Random Forest, learns from every real 15-minute reading how load actually responds to heat, weekends and breaks. Alone, the human estimates are rough and ignore weather: 24% error on real meters. Alone, the AI has nothing to tell building types apart except clock time, so it copies the metered buildings’ timing onto unmetered ones: 16%. Together: 9.7%. Errors are mean WAPE on the real campus with 30% of days hidden.
