05 · Limits and what’s next

What it can’t do yet, and what’s next

Today

  • Synthetic scores are optimistic, so we tested on real meters too
  • It doesn’t know another campus’s holidays
  • Simulate is a planning sketch, not a controls system

Next

  • Read each campus’s own calendar
  • Learn building schedules from data
  • Real billing periods, daylight saving, access control
Say these before the judges do. Our schedules were designed alongside the synthetic generator, which is why the real-data test matters. On the real campus the model didn’t know their holidays, and it expected the library to peak in the evening when it peaks at midday. Simulate assumes buildings can store cooling for a few hours. It rebuilds past months and is not a forecaster.
