How the AI is put to work

Model

Response

Outcome

Who drives the 4–8 PM peak. Pre-cool 25% earlier: peak −4.6%.

How the AI is used. The model is a Random Forest with 100 trees and 14 inputs: weekend, break, temperature and its 3-hour mean, cooling degrees, and the human schedule features. It needs no GPU and no cloud. Its response, for every bill-only building, is a 15-minute load curve that adds up to the bill, the accuracy it scored in testing, and a likely range built from those tested errors. The outcome for facilities: they can see which building types drive the 4–8 PM peak, and try what-ifs. Moving 25% of flexible cooling from 4–8 PM to 10 AM–4 PM cuts the campus peak by 4.6% in an average month. The next slides are the benchmarks.
