01 · Problem statement

You can’t cut a peak you can’t see

The 4–8 PM spike

Weekday demand jumps just as rooftop solar fades.

The missing meter

Some buildings report every 15 minutes. Others send one bill a month.

The flat split fails

Spreading a bill evenly hides the spike in the exact hours that matter.

Demand peaks 4–8 PM on weekdays while solar drops. Facilities can’t shift load they can’t see, and half the campus only sends a monthly bill. An even split of that bill wipes out the spike. They also need to know flexible load, like hostel cooling, from critical load, like labs, before touching anything.
