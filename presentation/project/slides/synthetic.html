On synthetic data we know the hidden truth, so we can score everything. The 4–8 PM energy error is 0.2 to 4.6 percent per building, and the peak hour is exactly right for four of five buildings. A few metered days help a lot: the Academic block goes from 11.3 to 7.5 percent with a quarter of days metered, while the flat split stays near 28.
