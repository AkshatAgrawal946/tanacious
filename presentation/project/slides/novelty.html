05 · Novelty

What’s new in our approach

Locked to the bill

Each month matches its bill within 0.01 kWh. Real readings stay unchanged.

Human knowledge as input

No clock time, so it carries over to building types with no meter.

Built to be checked

Upload meters, hide readings, rebuild and score them in the dashboard.

No new hardware

Monthly bills plus a few weeks of temporary pilot meters.

Four things set this apart. One: it is locked to the bill. Every rebuilt month adds up to its utility bill within 0.01 kWh, and real readings come back unchanged, so the output never contradicts what the campus already pays for. Two: human knowledge goes in as model input, not as a manual override. We left clock time out on purpose, which is why it carries over to building types that have no meter at all. Three: it is built to be checked. Any campus can upload its own meters, hide readings and see how close we get, against the flat split. Four: no new hardware. Monthly bills plus a few weeks of temporary pilot meters are enough.
